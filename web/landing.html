Everything your LLC needs

📄

Invoicing

Create invoices with PDF generation and Stripe payment links. Recurring retainers supported.

💰

Tax Estimation

Quarterly estimated tax for all 50 states. Schedule C and 1120-S support. Multi-state calculations.

🏦

Bank Reconciliation

Import OFX, QFX, CSV. Automated bank sync via Plaid. Match receipts to transactions.

🧾

Receipt Scanning

Snap a photo or upload a PDF. OCR extracts merchant, date, and total. AI-powered categorization.

🚗

Mileage Tracking

Log business trips at $0.70/mi. Auto-calculates your IRS deduction.

📊

Reports

Profit & Loss, expense breakdowns, Schedule C summary. Export to CSV.

⏱️

Time Tracking

Fetch billable hours from Toggl or Clockify. Auto-generate invoices from tracked time.

🤖

AI Categorization

OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama. Auto-categorize transactions as they're imported.

🔒

Plain Text Data

Your data lives in Beancount files. Git-versioned. Zero lock-in. Self-hosted on your own infrastructure.

How It Works

1

Deploy

Run one command to deploy on your VPS, Docker, Fly.io, or Railway. Or run locally.

2

Configure

Fill in your business details through the web setup wizard. Connect your bank or Stripe account.

3

Manage

Create invoices, scan receipts, import bank statements, estimate taxes — all from the web UI or CLI.
